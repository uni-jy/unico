# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Unico to be a welcoming project for people building personal, local-first music tools. We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept feedback when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Do not ask contributors to share private cookies, keys, playlists, or account data in public issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, cookies, or personal listening data.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who violate this code of conduct. Serious security or privacy concerns should be reported privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
